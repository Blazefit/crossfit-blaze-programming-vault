12 Days of Christmas 2026

December 14-25, 2026 | 2 Weeks | Focus: 12 Days of Christmas

Overview

The 12 Days of Christmas is a repeating CrossFit benchmark — each "day" adds one more round of the previous day's work, creating a cumulative chiper that builds to 78 total rounds of varying movements. It's typically programmed around the holiday season as a community challenge.

Structure

This cycle runs for 2 weeks leading into the benchmark:

Week 1: Movement Prep (Dec 14-20)

  • Introduce and teach each movement
  • Practice transitions
  • Build work capacity at lower volumes

Week 2: Accumulation & Benchmark (Dec 21-25)

  • Dec 21-24: Progressive daily volume leading to full benchmark
  • Dec 25: Full 12 Days of Christmas benchmark attempt
