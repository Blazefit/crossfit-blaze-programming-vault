Post-Murph Strength Build 2026

May 26 – July 19, 2026 | 8 Weeks | Focus: Strength Rebuild After Murph

Non-Negotiable Programming Rules

This cycle follows Programming-Rules.

Group Class Strength Format

All strength pieces must be written as timed blocks.

Correct format examples:

  • Every 3:00 for 15:00: perform 5 front squats, rest the remainder
  • Every 3:00 for 15:00: perform 8 RDL reps, rest the remainder
  • Every 4:00 for 20:00: perform 3 heavy deadlifts, rest the remainder

No open-ended “5 sets of 8, rest 90 sec” prescriptions for group class.

Conditioning Density

Regular conditioning intervals should keep athletes working 50–75% of the window.

For workouts with a time cap, the cap should not be twice as long as the likely finish time. A strong athlete should usually finish around 60–80% of the cap, and even elite athletes should not be regularly finishing in less than half the cap. If the cap is 9:00, the work should not be something Jason can finish in 5:00. Increase reps, rounds, load, or reduce the cap.

FormatTarget
AMRAP8–15 min total
EMOM35–50 sec work
E2MOM1:10–1:35 work
E3MOM2:00–2:20 work unless heavy/skill-based
Finisher8–12 min, not for time, local fatigue

If a decent athlete finishes an E3MOM in 60–90 sec, it is mistimed. Change it to E2MOM, AMRAP, or add work.

Bodybuilding Finishers

Strength-biased days should include a bodybuilding finisher unless the day is recovery-only, a long benchmark, or class time makes it impossible.

Finishers are:

  • 8–12 min
  • not for time
  • controlled reps
  • local muscle fatigue / pump
  • accessory or prehab focused
  • not a second metcon

Cycle Purpose

After Murph, the gym needs a pivot away from high-volume bodyweight fatigue and into structured strength rebuilding.

The goal is to make members feel strong, athletic, and durable again without burying them after Murph.

Primary goals:

  1. Build lower-body strength
  2. Build strict upper-body strength
  3. Rebuild posterior chain and trunk strength
  4. Keep conditioning present but controlled
  5. Add bodybuilding finishers for muscle, joints, and member buy-in
  6. Avoid accidental WOD density and daily beatdowns

Phase Structure

Phase 1 — Recover & Reset

Weeks 1–2 | May 26 – June 8

Theme: Recover from Murph, rebuild positions, reintroduce strength.

Rules:

  • No vest work
  • No Murph-style chippers
  • No high-volume pull-ups
  • No high-volume push-ups
  • No long running pieces
  • Strength is timed
  • Conditioning is low/moderate density
  • Bodybuilding finishers stay in, but avoid Murph fatigue patterns

Phase 2 — Build the Base

Weeks 3–4 | June 9 – June 22

Theme: Start measurable barbell progressions.

Weekly priorities:

  • Squat: timed 5s / 6s
  • Press: timed strict press or bench work
  • Hinge: timed deadlift or RDL work
  • Pull: strict pull + horizontal pulling volume
  • Conditioning: 8–15 min controlled pieces
  • Finishers: lower pump, upper pump, posterior chain, trunk

Phase 3 — Intensify

Weeks 5–6 | June 23 – July 6

Theme: Heavier strength, more power, still controlled WOD density.

Weekly priorities:

  • Squat: timed 3s / 4s
  • Hinge: timed heavy 3s or power pulls
  • Press: heavier strict press / bench
  • Pull: strict pull-up progressions, weighted options
  • Conditioning: 1–2 high days max per week
  • Finishers: bodybuilding balance so joints feel better, not worse

Phase 4 — Test & Consolidate

Weeks 7–8 | July 7 – July 19

Theme: Test strength without turning the week into chaos.

Rules:

  • No more than 2 true test days in a week
  • Conditioning tests are limited and planned
  • Strength tests still run on class clocks
  • Finishers may be reduced on max-effort days
  • Recovery placed between test exposures

Week 1 — Reset Week

↑ Back to top

Theme: Post-Murph recovery, movement quality, controlled strength Density: Low–Moderate Timing mix: recovery flow, E2MOM, continuous aerobic, short AMRAP, capped for-time, partner stations.

Monday — Murph Day / Recovery Day

↑ Back to top

If Monday is Murph Day, that is the event.

If this is the day after Murph:

Recovery Flow — 25:00 easy, not for score

  • 3:00 easy bike or row
  • 10 slow walking lunges
  • 10 band pull-aparts
  • 10 glute bridges
  • 30 sec couch stretch each side
  • 30 sec child’s pose lat stretch
  • 30 sec dead hang or ring stretch

Repeat easy until the clock ends.

Intent: Flush soreness, restore range of motion, no intensity.

No bodybuilding finisher today if directly after Murph. Recovery-only day.

Tuesday — Lower Body Reset

↑ Back to top

Focus: Tempo squat pattern + easy machine intervals

Strength — Every 3:00 for 15:00

  • 8 tempo goblet squats
  • Tempo: 3 sec down, 1 sec pause, smooth stand
  • Rest the remainder

Accessory — Every 2:00 for 8:00

  • 10 DB Romanian deadlifts
  • Rest the remainder

Conditioning — E2MOM x 5 / 10:00

Every 2:00:

  • 12/10 cal bike
  • 16 box step-ups
  • 12 sit-ups

Target finish: 1:20–1:40

Bodybuilding Finisher — 3 rounds, 7:00 cap

  • 15 banded hamstring curls
  • 12 standing calf raises
  • 20 sec Copenhagen plank each side

Controlled reps. Rest as needed. Not for score.

Wednesday — Aerobic Flush + Trunk

↑ Back to top

Focus: Sustainable engine, trunk, carries

Workout — 32:00 continuous, not for score

Move at conversational pace:

  • 2:00 row or bike
  • 100m farmer carry
  • 12 bird dogs
  • 12 med ball deadlifts
  • 30 sec front plank
  • 30 sec side plank each side

Intent: Low-density aerobic recovery. No redline.

Prehab Finisher — 6:00 flow

Move continuously with control:

  • 15 band pull-aparts
  • 10 slow empty-bar good mornings
  • 10 dead bugs
  • 20 sec squat hold

Keep restorative, not fatiguing.

Thursday — Upper Strict Strength

↑ Back to top

Focus: Strict press + horizontal pull

Strength — Every 3:00 for 15:00

  • 5 strict press
  • 8–10 ring rows after the press
  • Rest the remainder

Accessory — Every 90 sec for 9:00

Alternate stations:

  1. 10 single-arm DB rows each side
  2. 15 band face pulls
  3. 20 sec hollow hold

Two rounds through.

Conditioning — 9:00 AMRAP

  • 9/7 cal row
  • 9 kettlebell deadlifts
  • 6 burpees to target

Bodybuilding Finisher — Mechanical Drop Set, 6:00 cap

2 rounds:

  • 10 DB lateral raises
  • 10 partial lateral raises
  • 12 band triceps pressdowns

Local pump only. Not for time.

Friday — Posterior Chain Rebuild

↑ Back to top

Focus: RDL + short KB/row piece

Strength — Every 3:00 for 15:00

  • 8 barbell Romanian deadlifts
  • Moderate load, controlled tempo
  • Rest the remainder

Accessory — Every 2:30 for 10:00

  • 12 weighted glute bridges or hip thrusts
  • 20 sec side plank each side
  • Rest the remainder

Conditioning — For Time, 12:00 cap

4 rounds:

  • 15/12 cal row
  • 16 Russian KB swings
  • 16 reverse lunges total

Target: 8:30–11:00. Cap at 12:00.

Bodybuilding Finisher — 8-7-6 pump ladder

Not for time:

  • Hip thrusts: 8 reps, then 7 reps, then 6 reps
  • Banded hamstring curls: 16 reps, then 14 reps, then 12 reps

Rest as needed between sets.

Saturday — Partner Stations

↑ Back to top

Focus: Community, moderate sweat, simple movements

Partner Workout — 5 stations x 4:00 / 20:00 total

Partners rotate stations every 4:00. Split work however.

  1. Bike calories
  2. Light wall balls
  3. Box step-ups
  4. Sit-ups
  5. Farmer carry shuttle

Score: total reps/calories, but effort should stay moderate.

Intent: Move well, communicate, no high-volume push/pull.

Partner Pump Finisher — 6:00 alternating quality

Partners alternate full rounds:

  • 10 DB curls
  • 10 DB floor press
  • 50m light farmer carry

Pump work only, not for score.

Week 2 — Rebuild Patterns

↑ Back to top

Theme: Start training again, still no crushing volume Density: Low–Moderate Timing mix: timed strength, descending AMRAP, E2MOM, long continuous, interval carries, EMOM, partner chipper.

Monday — Squat Pattern

↑ Back to top

Focus: Front squat technique + controlled metcon

Strength — Every 3:00 for 15:00

  • 5 front squats
  • Moderate, technical load
  • Rest the remainder

Accessory — Every 2:00 for 10:00

  • 8 DB split squats each side
  • Rest the remainder

Conditioning — 10:00 descending AMRAP

  • 10 front rack reverse lunges, light
  • 10/8 cal bike
  • 10 sit-ups

After each full round, reduce lunges by 2 reps: 10, 8, 6, 4, 2. Bike and sit-ups stay the same.

Bodybuilding Finisher — 6:00 quad flush

As many quality sets as possible:

  • 8 DB Bulgarian split squats each side
  • 20 sec wall sit

Rest as needed. Not for score.

Tuesday — Upper Pull / Press

↑ Back to top

Focus: Strict pulling + DB pressing

Strength — Every 3:00 for 15:00

Choose one:

  • 3–5 strict pull-ups
  • 5 banded strict pull-ups
  • 8–10 ring rows

Then immediately:

  • 8 DB floor presses
  • Rest the remainder

Accessory — Every 90 sec for 9:00

Alternate:

  1. 10 DB rows each side
  2. 20 sec hollow hold + 10 dead bugs
  3. 12 band face pulls

Conditioning — E2MOM x 6 / 12:00

Every 2:00:

  • 12 DB push press, moderate
  • 12/10 cal bike
  • 8 burpees

Target finish: 1:25–1:45

Bodybuilding Finisher — 7:00 pump ladder

  • 12 DB hammer curls
  • 12 band triceps pressdowns
  • 15 band pull-aparts

Keep cycling with controlled reps for 7:00. Pump only.

Wednesday — Aerobic Base

↑ Back to top

Focus: Longer sustainable work

Workout — 36:00 continuous, easy-moderate pace

6 rounds at smooth pace:

  • 3:00 row/bike/ski
  • 20 walking lunges
  • 100m suitcase carry, switch at 50m
  • 12 light med ball cleans
  • 30 sec dead bug or hollow hold

Intent: Sustainable pace. No score chasing.

Trunk Finisher — Every 1:00 for 6:00

Alternate:

  • Minute 1: 30 sec suitcase hold right + 30 sec suitcase hold left
  • Minute 2: 12 reverse hypers or supermans
  • Minute 3: 15 slow calf raises

Repeat 2 rounds.

Thursday — Hinge + Carries

↑ Back to top

Focus: Deadlift technique, posterior chain, grip

Strength — Every 3:00 for 18:00

  • 3 deadlifts
  • Moderate load, perfect positions
  • Rest the remainder

Carry Block — Every 2:30 for 10:00

  • 100m farmer carry
  • Heavy but clean
  • Rest the remainder

Conditioning — 3 rounds for time, 12:00 cap

  • 20 KB swings
  • 20 box step-ups
  • 18/15 cal row

Target: 8:30–11:00. Cap at 12:00.

Bodybuilding Finisher — Every 90 sec for 6:00

  • 12 hip thrusts or glute bridges
  • 12 banded hamstring curls
  • Rest the remainder

Friday — Mixed Modal, Moderate Intensity

↑ Back to top

Focus: Hang power clean technique + short mixed piece

Skill / Strength — Every 1:30 for 12:00

  • 3 hang power cleans
  • Light-moderate, fast elbows
  • Rest the remainder

Conditioning — 12:00 EMOM

  • Minute 1: 8 hang power cleans, light
  • Minute 2: 12/10 cal bike
  • Minute 3: 10 burpees
  • Minute 4: rest

Repeat 3 rounds.

Work should take 35–50 sec on the working minutes.

Bodybuilding Finisher — 2 rounds, 7:00 cap

  • 12 DB high pulls, light
  • 12 DB curls
  • 15 band pull-aparts
  • 20 sec front rack stretch

Controlled quality.

Saturday — Partner Chipper

↑ Back to top

Focus: Light/moderate community workout

Partner Workout — For Time, 30:00 cap

Partners split work:

  • 80/64 cal row
  • 70 KB deadlifts
  • 60 box step-ups
  • 50 sit-ups
  • 40 ring rows
  • 30 burpees
  • 200m farmer carry together

Intent: Shared pacing, clean movement, sustainable effort. Cap at 30:00.

Partner Pump Finisher — 5:00 max-quality sets

Partners alternate:

  • 10 DB curls
  • 10 triceps extensions or band pressdowns
  • 20 sec plank

Not for score. Stop before sloppy reps.

↑ Back to top
