Blaze Winter Engine 2026-2027

Nov 30, 2026 – Feb 28, 2027 | 13 Weeks | 6 programmed days/week

Member-Facing Explanation

> “The weather is finally good enough to use the outside again. This cycle brings running back progressively while keeping strength, gymnastics, and CrossFit intensity in the program. The goal is a bigger engine without beating up knees, calves, or backs.”

Cycle Rules

  • Running returns gradually: short intervals first, then tempo pieces, then longer mixed workouts.
  • Every run has a machine option for scaling, weather, or injury.
  • Strength stays in the week, but the main adaptation is engine, pacing, and repeatability.
  • No sudden high-volume running spikes.
  • Bodybuilding/prehab finishers emphasize calves, tibialis, hamstrings, hips, trunk, and shoulders.
  • January/February can support Open-style fitness without making every Friday a test.

Monday — Squat Strength + Lower-Body Capacity

↑ Back to top

Intent: Phase 1 — Running Reintroduction + Aerobic Base. Main goal is squat strength + lower-body capacity without losing weekly balance.

Coach-Led Warm-Up — 8–10:00

2:00 easy machine, 10 air squats, 10 glute bridges, 10 walking lunges, 20 sec squat hold, 2 ramp sets for the squat pattern.

Strength / Skill — Timed Class Block

Every 3:00 for 18:00: 5 front squats or back squats, leave 2 reps in reserve, rest the remainder.

Conditioning

E2MOM x 6: 150m run or 10/8 cal bike, 10 goblet squats, 8 sit-ups

Bodybuilding / Prehab Finisher

Every 90 sec for 6:00: 12 goblet cyclist squats + 20 sec wall sit + 15 tibialis raises to support running volume.

Scaling

  • Elite: prescribed volume/load, unbroken where appropriate, hold target finish window.
  • Standard: moderate load, small planned breaks, finish within the intended cap/window.
  • Modified: reduce load/reps by 20–30%, use ring rows/knee raises/step-ups as needed.
  • Foundation: technique-first movement, machine calories, carries, and controlled reps. Run may be replaced by bike/row/ski calories to preserve the intended time domain.
