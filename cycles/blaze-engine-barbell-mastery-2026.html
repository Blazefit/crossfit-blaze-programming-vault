Blaze Engine & Barbell Mastery 2026

Aug 31, 2026 – Nov 29, 2026 | 13 Weeks | 6 programmed days/week

Member-Facing Explanation

> “We rebuilt strength after Murph, then turned that strength into speed and power. This cycle turns that into real CrossFit capacity: better barbell cycling, stronger gymnastics positions, smarter pacing, and more confidence in classic workouts.”

Cycle Rules

  • Every day includes a coach-led 8–10 minute warm-up.
  • Strength and skill work stays on the class clock.
  • Conditioning density increases gradually, but no back-to-back redline days.
  • Gymnastics volume builds from positions and strict strength before high-rep fatigue.
  • Running stays optional/limited; machines, carries, sleds, and indoor shuttles are the default until the winter weather cycle.
  • Bodybuilding finishers support joints, muscle, and member buy-in.

↑ Back to top

Intent: Phase 1 — Barbell Cycling Base + Gymnastics Positions. Main goal is squat strength + lower-body capacity without losing weekly balance.

Coach-Led Warm-Up — 8–10:00

2:00 easy machine, 10 air squats, 10 glute bridges, 10 walking lunges, 20 sec squat hold, 2 ramp sets for the squat pattern.

Strength / Skill — Timed Class Block

Every 3:00 for 18:00: 6 back squats or front squats, controlled down and fast stand, rest the remainder.

Conditioning

10:00 AMRAP: 10 wall balls, 10/8 cal bike, 10 sit-ups

Bodybuilding / Prehab Finisher

Every 90 sec for 6:00: 12 goblet cyclist squats + 20 sec wall sit

Scaling

  • Elite: prescribed volume/load, unbroken where appropriate, hold target finish window.
  • Standard: moderate load, small planned breaks, finish within the intended cap/window.
  • Modified: reduce load/reps by 20–30%, use ring rows/knee raises/step-ups as needed.
  • Foundation: technique-first movement, machine calories, carries, and controlled reps. Use bike/row/ski or indoor shuttles instead of outdoor running when heat or class flow requires it.
