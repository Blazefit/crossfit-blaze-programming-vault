Murph Prep 2026

2026-04-01 – 2026-05-25 | 8 weeks | 6 fully programmed training days/week

Completion Note

Every listed training day below is fully written with warm-up, timed strength/skill or workout structure, main conditioning, finisher/prehab when appropriate, intent, and scaling. No programmed day is hidden or collapsed.

CrossFit Blaze Class Rules

  • Every day includes a coach-led 8–10 minute warm-up.
  • Strength/skill blocks are on a class clock, not loose sets.
  • Main conditioning is programmed to last at least 8 minutes for strong athletes.
  • Finishers are controlled accessory/prehab/bodybuilding work, not second metcons.
  • Scaling preserves the intended stimulus.

Week 1 — Base + tissue tolerance

Theme: Base + tissue tolerance. Full 6-day training week.

Timing mix: squat/upper/aerobic/hinge/mixed/partner with varied clocks.

Wednesday 4/1 — 2:00 bike + squats, bridges, lunges, squat hold, barbell ramp.

Intent: Base + tissue tolerance. Controlled effort.

🔥 Coach-Led Warm-Up — 8–10:00

2:00 easy bike, 10 air squats, 10 glute bridges, 10 walking lunges, 20 sec squat hold, 2–3 empty-bar ramp sets.

💪 Strength / Skill

Every 3:00 for 15:00: 6 controlled front squats + 8 alternating step-ups, rest the remainder. Keep legs durable for Murph volume.

🚦 Conditioning

12:00 AMRAP: 12 wall balls, 12/10 cal bike, 10 box step-ups, 8 sit-ups.

🧱 Finisher

Every 90 sec for 9:00: 12 goblet cyclist squats + 20 sec wall sit, rest remainder.

📉 Scaling

  • Elite: Rx+ / advanced skill.
  • Standard: Rx class version.
  • Modified: -20–30% reps/load; simpler skill.
  • Foundation: technique first; light + simple.
