Blaze Power & Athletic Capacity 2026

July 20 – August 30, 2026 | 6 Weeks | Focus: Strength → Speed → Athletic Capacity

Member-Facing Explanation

> “We spent the last cycle rebuilding strength after Murph. Now we’re going to use it. This cycle turns strength into power — moving weight faster, sprinting harder, improving Olympic lifting, and becoming more athletic. You’ll still see strength work and bodybuilding finishers, but the focus shifts from grinding strength to explosive movement.”

Functional Goal

Turn the strength built in the Post-Murph cycle into usable athletic performance.

This cycle builds:

  1. Power — moving loads fast
  2. Olympic lifting skill — cleaner positions and better bar speed
  3. Sprint capacity — hard efforts with enough rest to repeat quality
  4. Athletic durability — jumps, landings, carries, unilateral work, trunk
  5. CrossFit transfer — faster cycling, better transitions, stronger short workouts

Non-Negotiable Rules

This cycle follows Programming-Rules.

  • Strength/power pieces are written as timed class blocks.
  • Conditioning intervals should keep athletes working 50–75% of the window unless the piece is true sprint-power or high skill.
  • Bodybuilding finishers stay in, but they support power and joint durability.
  • Timing formats must vary through the week.
  • No back-to-back redline days.
  • No random chaos — every day should have a clear adaptation.

Phase Structure

Weeks 1–2 — Learn Positions

Goal: Move well before moving fast.

Focus:

  • Landing mechanics
  • Hang power clean positions
  • Push press timing
  • Light power snatch exposure
  • Short sprint intervals with generous rest
  • Trunk and shoulder stability
  • Bodybuilding finishers focused on shoulders, posterior chain, arms, calves, trunk

Density:

  • Mostly moderate
  • One high day per week
  • No sloppy barbell cycling

Weeks 3–4 — Build Speed

Goal: Create more power and repeat it under fatigue.

Focus:

  • Faster bar speed
  • Power clean / power snatch cycling
  • Jump + lift pairings
  • Bike/row sprint repeats
  • Sled pushes / carries
  • Short AMRAPs and EMOMs with proper work windows

Density:

  • Moderate to moderate-high
  • Two harder days max per week
  • Wednesday stays lower-density

Weeks 5–6 — Express Power

Goal: Test power output and short-workout intensity without losing quality.

Focus:

  • Heavy but fast clean or snatch variations
  • Sprint repeatability
  • Short benchmark-style power workouts
  • Partner athletic capacity
  • One power retest
  • One short conditioning retest

Density:

  • Highest intensity of the cycle
  • Still no back-to-back redline days
  • Finishers reduced if the main dose is very high

6-Week Outline

Week 1 — Positions Before Speed

↑ Back to top

Theme: Learn to move fast without losing shape. Density: Moderate, one high-ish day. Timing mix: timed strength, E90 sec skill, E2MOM sprints, continuous aerobic, short AMRAP, partner stations.

Monday — Squat + Jump Mechanics

↑ Back to top

Goal: Maintain squat strength while teaching landing and jumping mechanics.

Power Prep — Every 1:00 for 6:00

  • 3 vertical jumps
  • Stick the landing for 2 sec

Strength — Every 3:00 for 15:00

  • 5 front squats
  • Moderate load, fast stand
  • Rest the remainder

Conditioning — 9:00 AMRAP

  • 8 goblet squats
  • 8/6 cal bike
  • 6 box jumps or step-down box jumps

Bodybuilding Finisher — 3 rounds, 7:00 cap

  • 10 DB Bulgarian split squats each side
  • 15 banded hamstring curls
  • 20 sec wall sit

Tuesday — Hang Clean Positions + Upper Pull

↑ Back to top

Goal: Build clean positions without fatigue-slop.

Skill — Every 90 sec for 12:00

  • 3 hang muscle cleans
  • 3 front rack elbows-through drills
  • 3 hang power cleans, light

Strength — Every 3:00 for 12:00

  • 5 strict pull-ups or 8 ring rows
  • 8 DB floor press
  • Rest the remainder

Conditioning — E2MOM x 5 / 10:00

Every 2:00:

  • 6 hang power cleans, light
  • 10/8 cal row
  • 6 burpees

Target finish: 1:10–1:30

Bodybuilding Finisher — 6:00 pump ladder

  • 10 DB hammer curls
  • 12 band face pulls
  • 12 band triceps pressdowns

Controlled reps.

Wednesday — Aerobic Base + Trunk

↑ Back to top

Goal: Keep the engine without dulling power work.

Workout — 34:00 continuous, conversational

  • 3:00 bike/row/ski
  • 100m suitcase carry
  • 12 med ball deadlifts
  • 10 step-ups each side
  • 30 sec dead bug or hollow hold

Prehab Finisher — Every 1:00 for 6:00

Alternate:

  • Minute 1: 15 band pull-aparts + 15 sec hang
  • Minute 2: 12 reverse hypers or supermans
  • Minute 3: 20 sec side plank each side

Repeat 2 rounds.

Thursday — Hinge Power + Bike Sprints

↑ Back to top

Goal: Fast hips, repeatable sprint output.

Power — Every 2:00 for 12:00

  • 5 kettlebell swings, heavy but snappy
  • 3 broad jumps
  • Rest the remainder

Strength — Every 3:00 for 12:00

  • 5 Romanian deadlifts
  • Moderate load, controlled down, fast up
  • Rest the remainder

Conditioning — Bike Sprint Intervals

Every 2:30 for 12:30:

  • 15/12 cal bike sprint
  • Rest the remainder

Target work: 45–65 sec. This is sprint-power, so longer rest is intentional.

Bodybuilding Finisher — Every 90 sec for 6:00

  • 12 hip thrusts
  • 12 banded hamstring curls
  • Rest the remainder

Friday — Power Metcon

↑ Back to top

Goal: Short, sharp CrossFit without turning into chaos.

Skill/Strength — Every 1:30 for 9:00

  • 2 push press
  • 3 fast burpees
  • Rest the remainder

Conditioning — 3 rounds for time, 10:00 cap

  • 10 push press, light/moderate
  • 12/10 cal row
  • 10 box jumps

Target: 6:30–9:00

Bodybuilding Finisher — Mechanical Drop Set, 6:00 cap

2 rounds:

  • 10 DB lateral raises
  • 10 partial lateral raises
  • 12 band triceps pressdowns

Saturday — Partner Athletic Capacity Stations

↑ Back to top

Goal: Team energy, simple movements, power endurance.

Partner Workout — 6 stations x 3:00 / 18:00 total

Partners rotate every 3:00. Split work however.

  1. Sled push or heavy farmer carry shuttle
  2. Bike calories
  3. Med ball cleans
  4. Box step-ups
  5. Ring rows
  6. Sit-ups

Partner Pump Finisher — 5:00 quality

Partners alternate:

  • 10 DB curls
  • 10 DB floor press
  • 50m farmer carry

Week 3 — Build Speed

↑ Back to top

Theme: Repeat power with better speed under mild fatigue.

  • Monday: timed squat + jump pairing, 10:00 mixed AMRAP, lower-body pump
  • Tuesday: clean technique + strict upper work, E2MOM barbell/bike intervals, upper pump
  • Wednesday: 34–38 min aerobic/carry/trunk continuous piece
  • Thursday: hinge power + sled/bike sprint intervals, posterior finisher
  • Friday: 8–10 min fast power metcon, shoulder/arm finisher
  • Saturday: partner stations with sled/carries/machines

Week 4 — Power Endurance

↑ Back to top

Theme: Express speed repeatedly without form breakdown.

  • Monday: front squat speed sets, box jump exposure, for-time capped leg/power piece
  • Tuesday: power snatch skill, strict press/pull, varied EMOM conditioning
  • Wednesday: low-density aerobic flush + trunk/prehab
  • Thursday: clean pull or deadlift speed, E3MOM sprint intervals, posterior pump
  • Friday: CrossFit power triplet, 11–13 min range, not chaotic
  • Saturday: partner chipper with carries/sleds/simple gymnastics

Week 5 — Express Power

↑ Back to top

Theme: Heavier but fast, shorter sharper conditioning.

  • Monday: squat heavy-fast triples on timed clock + jump contrast, reduced finisher
  • Tuesday: clean or snatch complex on timed clock + upper strength maintenance
  • Wednesday: zone 2, mobility-strength, trunk
  • Thursday: sprint-power test prep: bike/row/sled repeatability
  • Friday: short benchmark-style power workout, 6–9 min target
  • Saturday: partner athletic capacity, moderate-high but simple

Week 6 — Retest & Consolidate

↑ Back to top

Theme: Show power improvement without wrecking the body.

  • Monday: jump + squat speed retest, short controlled metcon, light lower finisher
  • Tuesday: Olympic lift skill retest or heavy-fast complex, upper pump
  • Wednesday: recovery aerobic + trunk
  • Thursday: sprint repeatability retest, posterior prehab
  • Friday: one power conditioning retest only
  • Saturday: community power celebration WOD + optional pump
