Blaze Winter Engine 2026-2027

Nov 30, 2026 – Feb 28, 2027 | 13 Weeks | 6 programmed days/week

Member-Facing Explanation

“The weather is finally good enough to use the outside again. This cycle brings running back progressively while keeping strength, gymnastics, and CrossFit intensity in the program. The goal is a bigger engine without beating up knees, calves, or backs.”

Cycle Rules

  • Running returns gradually: short intervals first, then tempo pieces, then longer mixed workouts.
  • Every run has a machine option for scaling, weather, or injury.
  • Strength stays in the week, but the main adaptation is engine, pacing, and repeatability.
  • No sudden high-volume running spikes.
  • Bodybuilding/prehab finishers emphasize calves, tibialis, hamstrings, hips, trunk, and shoulders.
  • January/February can support Open-style fitness without making every Friday a test.
